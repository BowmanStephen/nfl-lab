Week 5 previews

On the locked 2022–2025 holdout, this model went 48.8% against the spread (and 51.0% on totals) when it only bet edges of 2+ points on the side or 3+ on the total. That is below the 52.4% break-even line at −110. These week-5 picks are logged for the week-18 proof test — not bets, not advice.

Kickoff is Central Time. “Market” is the DraftKings line on ESPN, pulled 2026-10-05 01:09 CT. “Model” is the QB-adjusted projected margin (positive = home wins by that many) and projected total. A pick only appears when the locked rule fires. Every number is from the NFL Lab pipeline: nflverse through 2026 week 4, walk-forward ratings, starting-QB adjustment.

Thu Oct 8, 7:15 PM CT

Tampa Bay Buccaneers at Dallas Cowboys

TB +10

Market: DAL −10, total 47.5. Model: Dallas by 3.9, projected total 45.6.

Dallas’s offense has been excellent (about +0.20 EPA/play), but its defense has been a sieve (+0.19 allowed — higher is worse). Tampa’s offense has been bad (−0.16), and with Mayfield out the model only makes Dallas a small favorite. The market has Dallas −10; the model is closer to a field-goal game, so it prefers the Bucs getting that many points.

ESPN lists Baker Mayfield Out, so this projection’s Jalon Daniels look is the right starter assumption; Dak Prescott is the Dallas side.

Newcomer tip — EPA/play Every snap has a “fair” point value before the ball is snapped. EPA is how much the play moved that number. Dallas’s +0.20 offense means it’s gaining roughly two-tenths of a point per snap on average; that’s excellent.

Spread pick: TB +10 — edge 6.1 pts
