Week 5 previews

Updated Wed Oct 7, 6:30 PM CT. Vegas lines are DraftKings, from ESPN's scoreboard. Starting quarterbacks come from ESPN's depth charts and injury reports, checked against our own data.

Our model has not beaten Vegas yet. In a test on 2022 to 2025 games, it got 48.8% of spread picks and 51.0% of over/under picks right. A standard bet needs 52.4% to break even. That test only counted gaps of 2+ points on the margin or 3+ on the total.

Our official week 5 picks were locked Monday Oct 5 and are on the home page. These previews do not change them, and none of this is betting advice. Kansas City and Carolina are on bye.

Kickoff times are Central. "Vegas says" is the DraftKings line on ESPN, pulled Oct 7 at 6:30 PM CT. "Our model says" is our projected winner and margin, adjusted for the starting quarterbacks, plus our projected total points. We list a lean only when the gap clears our locked rule. Our numbers use nflverse data through 2026 week 4 and ratings built only from games already played.

Thu Oct 8, 7:15 PM CT

Tampa Bay Buccaneers at Dallas Cowboys

Tampa Bay keeps it within 8 (TB +8.5)

Vegas says DAL −8.5, total 47.5. Our model says Cowboys by 3.5, projected total 45.6.

We think Dallas wins, but by less than Vegas does. The Cowboys' offense has been excellent (about +0.20 EPA/play) and the defense has been leaky (+0.19 allowed, where higher is worse). Tampa's offense has been bad (−0.16). With Baker Mayfield out, the model only makes Dallas a 3.5-point favorite.

Baker Mayfield is Out (dislocated thumb; ESPN return date Oct 25). The depth chart still lists him as QB1, and the nflverse week-5 report leaves his status blank. The schedule lists Jalon Daniels, so this projection reruns the QB adjustment with Daniels. Dak Prescott starts for Dallas.

Newcomer tip: EPA/play Every snap has a fair point value before the ball is snapped. EPA is how much the play moved that number. Dallas's +0.20 offense means it is gaining about two-tenths of a point per snap on average.

Preview lean on the spread: TB +8.5, a 5.0-point gap.
